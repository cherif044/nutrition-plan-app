How it works

Three steps from stats to a full day of food.

1

Enter your inputs

Weight, activity level, meals per day and your goal — the same panel that stays pinned at the top of the plan.

2

Get allowed ranges

Pinch calculates a calorie target plus allowed ranges for protein, carbs and fat — for the day and for each meal.

3

Build the day your way

Meals arrive fitted to those ranges. Flip meals, swap single foods, alternate produce — the totals follow along.
